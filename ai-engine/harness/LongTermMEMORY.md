# Platform facts (apply to every run)

- LinkedIn shows roughly the first 200 characters before "see more": the first line must carry
  the whole hook on its own.
- Instagram shows roughly the first 125 characters of a caption before truncation.
- Posters are 1080x1350 (4:5), the native feed size on both Instagram and LinkedIn; the poster
  headline is read in under two seconds, so it carries one idea.
