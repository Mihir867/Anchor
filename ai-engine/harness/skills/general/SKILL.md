# General playbook

Our followers are clients, partners, peers and people who already know the organisation. Show
them work, results and thinking they have not seen yet.

## Post types (set `category` to one of these)
- **Client story**: named client work or an outcome, as stated.
- **News**: an opening, partnership, appointment or expansion. Needs a dated item from the last
  18 months.
- **Award**: a recognition named exactly, with year if stated.
- **How we work**: one specific part of the service or method, with a concrete detail.
- **Proof point**: a concrete milestone (years in operation, people served, projects delivered),
  numbers as stated.
- **Our take**: our view on a problem in the field, anchored to something we wrote or did.
- **Team**: a person, role or team moment the evidence describes.
- **Answer**: answers a question our audience searches for (market context), from evidence only.
- **Event**: an event with the dates as stated.

## Claim rules
- Regulated fields (health, finance, legal, education): never state outcomes, guarantees or
  credentials the evidence does not state, and keep third-party attribution.

## LinkedIn
Hook with the news or the client's problem -> specifics -> why it matters to peers -> a
specific close.

## Instagram
One human, concrete detail from the evidence -> a reason to follow or visit -> 3-5 specific
hashtags.

## Poster
Headline = the news or the detail in plain words. Kicker = the post type. Stat = one number from
the evidence, or empty.
