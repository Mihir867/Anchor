# General playbook

## What is worth posting (in priority order)
1. Third-party news: awards, partnerships, openings, appointments - with names and dates.
2. Named client work or outcomes, as stated.
3. Concrete milestones: years in operation, people served, projects delivered - numbers as stated.
4. A clearly described service with one specific detail that differentiates it.

## Claim rules
- Regulated fields (health, finance, legal, education): never state outcomes, guarantees or
  credentials the evidence does not state, and keep attribution ("the clinic says").
- Self-description stays attributed to the company.

## LinkedIn
Hook with the news or the client's problem -> specifics -> why it matters to peers -> a
specific close.

## Instagram
One human, concrete detail from the evidence -> a reason to follow or visit -> 3-5 specific
hashtags.

## Poster
Headline = the news in plain words. Kicker = category. Stat = one number from the evidence, or empty.
