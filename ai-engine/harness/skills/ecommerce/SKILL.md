# E-commerce playbook

Our followers are customers and fans who already know the brand. They respond to product
detail, real reviews, how to get more out of what they bought and the story behind the product.

## Post types (set `category` to one of these)
- **Collection drop**: a launch, collection or collaboration with named partners. Needs a dated
  item from the last 18 months.
- **Product detail**: one product's specifics shoppers decide on: material, ingredient, fit,
  origin, price as listed.
- **How to use**: care, styling, routine or usage tips the evidence gives.
- **Review spotlight**: a customer review quoted verbatim with the rating and count as shown; it
  speaks for that reviewer, not all customers.
- **Behind the product**: how or where it is made, sourced or designed, as stated.
- **Credential**: a certification (B Corp, organic, cruelty-free) named exactly.
- **Store news**: stores, stockists or pop-ups with named places and dates.
- **Milestone**: funding, growth or expansion from press, numbers as stated.
- **Answer**: answers a question shoppers search for (market context), from the evidence only.

## Claim rules for this industry
- No scarcity or urgency ("selling out", "last chance") unless the evidence states stock or an
  end date.
- Health, skincare or sustainability claims only as worded by the evidence; never upgrade
  "made with recycled materials" to "sustainable" or "eco-friendly".
- Prices with currency as listed; discounts only with the terms shown.

## LinkedIn
Audience: retail and brand operators, buyers, partners, investors. Shape: hook with the
business move (launch, collab, expansion, a customer insight) -> the specifics -> why it
matters for the category -> a specific close.

## Instagram
Audience: shoppers and fans. Product-first and tactile: one concrete detail (material, fit,
ingredient, the reviewer's words) -> a reason to save or shop -> 3-5 product- or
category-specific hashtags.

## Poster
Headline = the product, the tip or the launch in plain words. Kicker = collection, collaborator
or post type. Stat = rating, price or one number from the evidence.

## When evidence is thin
Product pages alone support a Product detail post and, with reviews, a Review spotlight. Do not
invent a launch.
