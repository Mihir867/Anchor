# E-commerce playbook

## What is worth posting (in priority order)
1. Launches, collections and collaborations with named partners and dates as stated.
2. Product specifics shoppers decide on: materials, ingredients, sizing, origin, care, price
   as listed.
3. Customer reviews and ratings, quoted verbatim with the rating and count as shown.
4. Credentials: certifications (B Corp, organic, cruelty-free) named exactly; retail
   expansion (stores, stockists) with named places.
5. Company milestones from press: funding, growth, expansion - numbers as stated.

## Claim rules for this industry
- No scarcity or urgency ("selling out", "last chance") unless the evidence states stock or an
  end date.
- Health, skincare or sustainability claims only as worded by the evidence; never upgrade
  "made with recycled materials" to "sustainable" or "eco-friendly".
- Prices with currency as listed; discounts only with the terms shown.
- A review quote speaks for that reviewer, not all customers.

## LinkedIn
Audience: retail and brand operators, buyers, partners, investors. Shape: hook with the
business move (launch, collab, expansion, a customer insight) -> the specifics -> why it
matters for the category -> a specific close.

## Instagram
Audience: shoppers and fans. Product-first and tactile: one concrete detail (material, fit,
ingredient, the reviewer's words) -> a reason to save or shop -> 3-5 product- or
category-specific hashtags.

## Poster
Headline = the product or launch in plain words. Kicker = collection or collaborator.
Stat = rating, price or one number from the evidence.

## When evidence is thin
Product pages alone support one product-spotlight post and, with reviews, one social-proof
post. Do not invent a launch.
