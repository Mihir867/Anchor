# SaaS playbook

## What is worth posting (in priority order)
1. A named customer outcome with a measured result (time saved, cost cut, revenue, latency,
   adoption), ideally from a case study or press article.
2. A launch or general-availability release with a concrete capability (what it does, for whom).
3. Funding, acquisition or partnership with named parties and amounts as stated.
4. Integrations with named platforms, security/compliance credentials (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA)
   exactly as named, open-source milestones (stars, contributors) with numbers as stated.
5. A pricing change or free tier only when the pricing evidence shows it.
Skip generic value propositions ("save time", "work smarter") unless a fact makes them concrete.

## Claim rules for this industry
- "Free" only if the pricing evidence shows a free tier or trial; quote the plan name.
- A metric belongs to whoever the evidence attributes it to (a customer, a benchmark, the
  company); keep that attribution in the post.
- Compliance: name the exact standard; never upgrade "SOC 2 Type I" to "Type II" or imply
  certification from "compliant" language.
- Customer logos on the site prove a customer relationship exists, not any outcome.

## LinkedIn
Audience: CTOs, engineering and product leaders, operators who buy software.
Shape: hook line with the outcome or the tension it resolves -> 2-4 short paragraphs on what
changed, how, and the proof -> one line on who should care -> a specific close (a question
about the reader's own setup, or where to read the case study). Technical precision beats
adjectives.

## Instagram
Audience: builders, designers, technical creators. Lead with the craft or the product moment;
2-4 short lines; one concrete detail; 3-5 specific hashtags (#devtools, #api, not #success).

## Poster
Headline = the outcome or the release in plain words. Kicker = category or customer name.
Stat = the single strongest metric, copied exactly.

## When evidence is thin
If the site offers only feature copy, prefer one well-grounded launch or capability post over
three vague ones, and attribute claims to the company.
